# DoseBuddy 2.0 — Milestone 5

- Fixes Insights so explicitly resolved future test doses count immediately while unresolved future doses remain excluded.
- Adds Routine Tools with reminder preferences, refill alerts, routine grouping, and travel-mode behavior settings.
- Adds first-class PRN/as-needed dose logging with reason, amount, notes, supply decrement, and timeline-compatible DoseEvents.
- Adds refill supply update action on medication details.
- Adds low-supply intelligence to Today.
- Adds Today visibility for as-needed medications.

Note: travel settings are planning/preferences in this milestone; they do not automatically alter prescribed dose times. Follow-up reminder preferences are stored for the notification-engine expansion.
